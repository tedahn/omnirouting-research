# Grounded Research Observatory

- **Purpose:** turn uncertain market, business, enterprise, technology, or industry information into a decision with auditable evidence.
- **Boundary:** do not become a passive news archive, content summarizer, or confirmation engine.
- **North star:** decisions whose key claims, alternatives, uncertainty, and update triggers were explicit before action.

## Human runbook

- Keep separate chats for decision desk, evidence collection, quantitative scenarios, countercase, decision memo, freshness watch, and calibration review.
- Pre-register the decision and belief before retrieval. Red-team citation entailment, source independence, base rates, calculations, incentives, and scope before action.
- At resolution, distinguish decision quality from luck; update forecasts and add material misses to the eval set.

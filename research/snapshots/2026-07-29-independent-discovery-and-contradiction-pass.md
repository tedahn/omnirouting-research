## Bottom line

The Stage 1 saturation gate **does not pass**. Three independent lanes found material categories absent from the first-pass taxonomy and outcomes absent from the first-pass ledger:

1. partial-trajectory routing for multi-turn agents;
2. deployment-realistic chosen-arm feedback and online SLA control;
3. multimodal and generative-media routing;
4. joint model and response-budget selection;
5. decision-aware anti-collapse routing;
6. router control-plane security and safety-tier integrity;
7. multi-round router-agent selection and aggregation; and
8. router-level, OOD, rare-expert, and evaluation-artifact diagnostics.

The product lane also found a live [Runway Media Router](https://runway.com/news/company-news/introducing-runway-media-router), upgraded Microsoft and NVIDIA status, and added Vercel plus agent-handoff boundaries. The contradiction lane found measured evidence that routing labels can be corrupted by judge, truncation, and format artifacts; that some routers collapse toward the dominant or strongest model; and that broad benchmark performance can fail to beat the best fixed model.

No theory card or scientific experiment was created because discovery uncovered decision-changing evidence.

## Strongest contradiction map

| Prior intuition | Counterevidence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| More budget lets a router choose better | EquiRouter reports stronger-model collapse as budget increases | Track model call share, not only aggregate utility |
| Router labels approximate capability | Unsolvability Ceiling finds judge, truncation, and format artifacts can dominate labels | Audit label construction before training or estimating oracle headroom |
| A router that saves cost is safe enough | DSC and adversarial rerouting show category shortcuts and control-plane manipulation | Apply safety/privacy eligibility and integrity checks before quality-cost selection |
| Broad benchmarks imply robust value | LLMRouterBench finds several routers fail Best Single and perform poorly on rare experts | Require Best Single, rare-expert, OOD, and oracle-gap baselines |
| Prompt-only difficulty is sufficient | SWE-Router reports a material benefit from partial execution trajectories | Compare prompt-only against delayed, state-aware escalation |
| Offline full-information labels match deployment | BaRP frames chosen-arm feedback as the deployable observation regime | Preregister exploration propensities and counterfactual estimators |

## Remaining high-value gaps

1. Independent, customer-grade production outcomes remain scarce.
2. Tail latency, cache loss, retry amplification, and current all-in pricing are rarely reported together.
3. Live model-pool churn and temporal drift remain weakly tested.
4. Safety, privacy, moderation, and data-policy changes across fallback destinations need direct evaluation.
5. Agentic routing lacks a common multi-turn benchmark spanning coding, tools, handoffs, recovery, and total trajectory cost.
6. Router-level online evaluation still faces selection bias, participation bias, and missing counterfactual outcomes.
7. The expanded entity universe has not yet undergone a second exact-location and duplicate audit.

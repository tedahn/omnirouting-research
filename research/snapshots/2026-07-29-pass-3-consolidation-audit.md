## No-addition test and stop decision

Formulation A searched from the unresolved failure surfaces rather than known project names:

- model-pool churn, drift, cache loss, retry amplification, and tail latency;
- privacy, moderation, safety policy, and fallback destinations;
- multi-turn agent routing, recovery, handoff, and total trajectory cost; and
- counterfactual online evaluation, selection bias, and propensity controls.

It found [Conformal LLM Routing with Distribution-Free Safety Guarantees](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-srw.70/), ACL Student Research Workshop, July 2026, pages 791-799. The paper reports a prompt-embedding logistic gate with Clopper-Pearson conformal calibration. On GSM8K and MMLU with a Mixtral-8x7B/GPT-4 pair, it maintains the target alpha across 0.05 to 0.50 within the stated delta tolerance while a validation-tuned baseline crosses the GSM8K boundary.

Disposition: material candidate, not yet ledgered. It may extend MTH-003 rather than create a new decision-locus family, but its distribution-free violation-rate guarantee and bound-compliance outcome are absent from current structured evidence. Formulation B was not run because the Stage-1 stop rule requires termination after a material addition.

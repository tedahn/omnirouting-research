# Research brief: company evolution of omni routing and dynamic model selection

- **Status:** Ready for bounded Stage 1 after owner confirmation
- **Decision owner:** Research lead (identity unresolved)
- **Decision deadline:** No calendar date supplied; first gate is before company profiling
- **Prepared:** 2026-07-27

## Decision to support

Determine which omni-routing and dynamic-model-selection directions merit deeper investigation, monitoring, or representative experimentation based on company activity, disclosed methods, studied outcomes, limitations, and plausible future scenarios.

## Scope

- **Included:** global companies, labs, and open projects with public primary evidence concerning routing across models, providers, modalities, tools, agents, regions, or execution paths; methods, outcomes, failures, constraints, and two-horizon forecasts.
- **Default period:** 2023–present, extended earlier only for foundational methods.
- **Excluded:** generic AI directories, vendor purchasing recommendations, popularity rankings, marketing-only performance claims, proprietary reconstruction, private data, and implementation work.
- **Minimum evidence:** one qualifying primary artifact per profiled entity; explicit method fields; outcome claims with metric, baseline, workload, setting, result, and limitations; independent/cross-case support for material synthesis.
- **Stop condition:** stop or narrow when two discovery passes add no material method category, evidence cannot cross a required threshold, the approved budget is reached, or the research no longer changes prioritization.

## Starting beliefs

Starting hypothesis, not a fact: routing may be evolving from fixed provider/model rules toward adaptive, multi-objective selection across broader execution paths. The research must actively seek evidence that this framing is incomplete, commercially overstated, operationally impractical, or constrained by governance and switching costs.

## Decisive questions

1. Which companies and projects have attributable evidence of work in scope, and which are marketing-only leads?
2. What decision locus, signals, mechanism, objectives, learning loop, execution pattern, and fallback does each approach use?
3. What outcomes were studied under what baselines, workloads, metrics, settings, and limitations—and which results are independently supported?
4. Where do approaches fail or remain uneconomic, brittle, difficult to govern, or non-comparable?
5. Which future scenarios are mechanistically plausible, what would falsify them, and what leading indicators deserve monitoring?

## Method for this phase

Follow `research/METHODOLOGY-omni-routing.md` and `research/RESEARCH_PLAN-2026-07-27.md`. Discover broadly by architecture and problem, profile only with qualifying primary evidence, normalize outcomes only when settings permit, search deliberately for contradiction and negative evidence, and separate forecasts from current observations.

## Deliverable gate

The initial cycle is complete when the company universe and exclusions are auditable, the stratified profiles and method taxonomy meet their evidence gates, outcome comparisons expose non-comparability, the countercase is represented, future scenarios have falsifiers, and a reviewed prioritization or experiment backlog names reversal evidence.

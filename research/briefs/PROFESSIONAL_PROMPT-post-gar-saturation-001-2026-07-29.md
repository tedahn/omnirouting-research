# Professional prompt — post-GAR saturation formulation 001

## Purpose

Test whether Stage 1 remains open after integrating GAR by searching a deliberately different mechanism and outcome surface: strategic actors, provider incentives, user choice, and welfare in multi-model routing.

This is a discovery test, not an experiment, theory promotion, product recommendation, or permission to activate the continuous workflow.

## Human gate and authority

- Gate: G2 evidence acceptance under `decisions/GATE-001-gar-public-source-integration.md`.
- Human decision owner: workspace requester acting as research lead.
- LLM role: retrieve public metadata, inspect at most one material primary artifact, compare it with the ledgers, and draft a stop recommendation.
- LLM may not approve its own evidence, promote a theory, authorize an experiment, contact authors, use private or paid data, change production systems, or activate a scheduler.

## Frozen question

Does public primary research expose a mechanism or measured-outcome category absent from the current routing taxonomy in any of these areas?

1. Incentive-compatible or market-based allocation among model providers.
2. Strategic provider behavior, payments, auctions, or mechanism design.
3. User choice, abandonment, re-prompting, cascade welfare, or heterogeneous user utility.
4. Worst-group or distributional welfare that is not merely expert-load balancing.

## Search formulations

Run these as one independent formulation:

- `LLM routing incentive compatible provider market auction mechanism design`
- `large language model routing user choice welfare abandonment reprompt cascade`
- `LLM router worst group welfare fairness model selection`

## Inclusion and exclusion

Include only a primary paper, official artifact, or source repository with a concrete routing mechanism or measured outcome. Exclude generic surveys, marketing synthesis, duplicated current families, model marketplaces without routing allocation logic, and fairness claims limited to expert-load balance.

## Evidence and context contract

- Use `context-packs/CTX-001-gar-integration.md` and the current CSV ledgers.
- Label metadata leads as leads until the primary artifact is opened.
- Record exact artifact version, location, unit, workload, baseline, assumptions, negative evidence, and unresolved gaps.
- Do not convert energy, carbon, money, provider payment, or user welfare into each other.

## Budget and stop rules

- Budget: at most two additional primary-source opens or 30 minutes; metadata search does not authorize extra primary opens.
- Stop immediately after the first material new mechanism or measured-outcome category.
- Also stop on ambiguous primary evidence, crossed data boundary, source domination, or budget exhaustion.
- If material novelty appears, do not run formulation 002. Leave Stage 1 open and propose exactly one bounded next action.

## Output contract

Return:

1. Search strings and candidates screened.
2. Primary artifact and exact locations, if opened.
3. Existing-family comparison.
4. Material-addition or no-addition decision with falsifier.
5. Stop reason, budget used, unresolved gaps, and exactly one next human decision.

Success is an auditable stop decision. It is not evidence that dynamic routing is superior.

# Next action 001 — build the bounded company and project universe

- **Status:** Ready after plan confirmation
- **Owner:** Research lead (identity unresolved)
- **Deadline:** Before the first company profile is accepted
- **Approval needed:** Named decision owner
- **Decision unlocked:** Which entities and method strata enter the evidence-grounded profiling stage

## Do

Run two bounded discovery passes using architecture- and problem-oriented queries across official technical documentation, engineering publications, repositories, papers, model/system cards, conference material, and attributable builder sources. Deduplicate companies, products, labs, and projects; record candidate entities, qualifying artifacts, routing relationship, provisional method stratum, inclusion status, exclusions, and search gaps in `company-landscape.csv`, `sources.csv`, and a dated discovery snapshot.

## Done when

- The owner confirms the methodology, public-source boundary, and effort limit.
- Every candidate has an entity type, discovery source, provisional routing relationship, and inclusion status.
- Every profiled candidate has at least one qualifying Tier A or B primary artifact.
- Exclusions and marketing-only leads remain visible with reasons.
- The universe covers distinct decision loci, mechanisms, objectives, and company/entity types or records the coverage gap.
- Two independent discovery passes add no material method category, or the approved budget stops expansion.

## Verify

Review the search log, company ledger, source ledger, inclusion/exclusion decisions, duplicate handling, primary-artifact threshold, and strata coverage. Run `EVAL-001` before accepting the first three profiles.

## Stop rule

Do not start profile synthesis, rank companies, infer production adoption, or make a future prediction if the owner/budget remains unresolved, a candidate lacks qualifying evidence, search results are dominated by one commercial source, or the data boundary would be crossed.

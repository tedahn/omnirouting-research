# Workspace charter

- **Workspace:** OmniRouting Research Observatory
- **Workspace type:** Grounded Research Observatory
- **Status:** Active plan — execution awaiting owner confirmation
- **Initialized:** 2026-07-27
- **Owner:** Research lead (identity unresolved)
- **Review trigger:** Owner assignment, Stage 1 completion, material scope change, first synthesis, or foundry migration

## Mission

Determine which omni-routing and dynamic-model-selection directions deserve deeper investigation, ongoing monitoring, or representative experimentation by comparing company activity, disclosed mechanisms, studied outcomes, limitations, and calibrated future scenarios.

## Current scope

- **Included:** companies, labs, and open projects with public evidence related to routing among models, providers, modalities, tools, agents, regions, or execution paths; their disclosed methodologies; evaluation designs and outcomes; negative evidence and constraints; and dated future scenarios.
- **Research decision:** choose which architecture families, evidence gaps, and future signals merit deeper investigation, monitoring, or representative experiments.
- **Unresolved:** named owner, calendar deadline, time/cost budget, target model and tools for research execution, and whether later work will support a build-versus-buy or product decision.
- **Excluded by default:** generic AI company directories, popularity rankings, vendor recommendations, marketing-only outcome claims, private/customer data, unbounded capability exploration, product delivery, production changes, and external commitments.

## Evidence standard

Use the evidence states and source tiers in `project/handbook/EVIDENCE_GOVERNANCE.md`. Keep observation, source claim, inference, forecast, opinion, and recommendation separate. Record exact supporting locations, counterevidence, `as_of`, and `refresh_by` for material claims.

## Authority

- Research and drafting are allowed within the approved scope and data boundary.
- Publishing, contacting people, purchasing, uploading sensitive material, changing systems, or making legal, financial, security, medical, or externally binding decisions requires explicit approval.
- When no named owner exists, role-level actions may prepare artifacts but may not cross an approval gate.

## Sources of truth

- Stable workspace policy: this charter and `project/PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
- Current operating state: `CURRENT_STATE.md`.
- Evidence and calibration: active ledgers under `research/`.
- Decisions: reviewed records under `research/decisions/`.
- Volatile evidence: dated records under `research/snapshots/`.

## Review cadence

- Weekly while active: unresolved high-value questions, claim freshness, contradictions, and next action.
- Monthly: source-monitoring value, forecast resolution, protocol failures, and stale evidence.
- Release-triggered: reopen claims affected by material model, product, policy, or market changes.
